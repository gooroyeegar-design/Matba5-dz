# Recipe content expansion policy

MATBAK DZ should grow to thousands of recipes without copying protected editorial text or photographs.

## Allowed ingestion

- Public-domain and openly licensed recipe datasets, with provenance and license retained.
- Commercial recipe feeds where MATBAK DZ has an appropriate license.
- User-submitted recipes, subject to moderation and contributor terms.
- Factual recipe data (ingredients, quantities, temperatures, timings and methods) researched from public sources and rewritten into original MATBAK DZ wording where legally appropriate.
- Original recipes generated for the application.
- Images from sources whose license explicitly permits reuse, or original/generated images produced for MATBAK DZ.

## Not allowed

Do not bulk-copy recipe article text, creator commentary, or photographs from YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, blogs, or recipe sites without permission/license. A public URL does not by itself grant redistribution rights.

## Provenance

Every imported record must retain source URL, source name, license, attribution requirements and import timestamp. If an image is not redistributable, the record must use an original/licensed/generated image instead.

## Scaling target

The backend must support at least 5,000 records immediately and remain capable of 100,000+ records through server-side pagination, indexed filtering, search and lazy image loading. Recipe records must not be bundled into the APK.
